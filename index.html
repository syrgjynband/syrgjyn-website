About Syrgjyn

Founded in Tirana

Syrgjyn was founded in Tirana, Albania, in 1999. The band’s story is tied to the local underground rock and metal scene.

The Alkimia years, 2005–2011

From September 2005 to August 2011, Syrgjyn performed every Saturday night at Alkimia in Tirana. During that period the band played as two musicians with a drum machine, often performing sets lasting three to four hours.

The underground legacy

Those evenings covered rock, punk, heavy metal, thrash, gothic metal, death metal and black metal. The band describes these performances as a formative part of its identity and its connection with Tirana’s music community.
